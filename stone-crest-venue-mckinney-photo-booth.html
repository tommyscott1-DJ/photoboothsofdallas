What Makes This Venue Special

Stone Crest Venue in New Hope near McKinney stands out for its hilltop setting and sweeping views of the surrounding countryside. The venue combines rustic, modern and industrial touches, with indoor and outdoor ceremony options and a prominent stone fireplace.

Its dramatic views provide a beautiful backdrop for the ceremony, while a photo booth offers a different kind of memory during the reception. A Classic Enclosed Photo Booth is especially fitting for guests who enjoy the nostalgic feel of stepping behind a curtain and leaving with real photo strips.
