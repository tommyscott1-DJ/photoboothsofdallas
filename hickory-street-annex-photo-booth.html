Photo Booth Rentals for Hickory Street Annex

Planning a wedding, party or special event at Hickory Street Annex? Photo Booths of Dallas offers a real photo booth experience for your guests, with printed keepsakes to take home.

What Makes This Venue Special

Hickory Street Annex offers a distinctive alternative to the traditional ballroom. Its historic industrial buildings near downtown Dallas feature enormous factory windows, exposed steel trusses, wood ceilings and a courtyard, creating a memorable mix of urban character and romantic possibilities.

The natural light and architectural details make this a particularly appealing setting for couples who enjoy industrial-chic style. A vintage-style enclosed photo booth would complement the historic surroundings, while giving guests their own little space to make printed photo-strip memories.
