Photo Booth Rentals for Rooftop Event Spot

Planning a wedding, party or special event at Rooftop Event Spot? Photo Booths of Dallas offers a real photo booth experience for your guests, with printed keepsakes to take home.

What Makes This Venue Special

Rooftop Event Spot brings a different kind of celebration to Historic Downtown Plano. Located above the original Plano Ice House, it combines indoor event space with an outdoor patio, offering an urban setting that works for weddings, private parties and corporate gatherings.

The indoor-outdoor layout and downtown atmosphere make this an especially fun option for hosts who want something beyond a conventional banquet hall. A photo booth gives guests another reason to mingle, laugh and leave with a printed keepsake after enjoying the rooftop setting.

Classic Enclosed Photo Booth

Step behind the curtain for a traditional photo booth experience with privacy, fun props and photo strips printed at your event. Explore our Classic Enclosed Photo Booth.

Open-Air Photo Booth

Our Open-Air Photo Booth is another option for guests who love group pictures and easy photo sharing. Both booth styles include a friendly attendant and personalized photo-strip design.

Pricing & Booking

Photo booth rentals start at $450 for up to 3 hours, with additional time at $100 per hour. We can discuss your event details, venue logistics and the booth style that works best for your celebration.
