What Makes This Venue Special

Oak + Ivy stands out for its French Country-inspired white barn in Aubrey, framed by mature oak trees and meadow views. The Great Hall's tall ceilings, chandeliers and generous windows give celebrations an elegant, airy character without losing the warmth of a country setting.

The covered pavilion and stone fireplace provide another appealing backdrop for ceremonies or cocktail-hour gatherings. For a reception here, a Classic Enclosed Photo Booth could offer guests a cozy, nostalgic experience alongside the venue's refined barn atmosphere.
