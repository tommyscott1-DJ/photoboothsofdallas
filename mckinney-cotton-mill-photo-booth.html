Photo Booth Rentals for The McKinney Cotton Mill

Planning a wedding, party or special event at The McKinney Cotton Mill? Photo Booths of Dallas offers a real photo booth experience for your guests, with printed keepsakes to take home.

What Makes This Venue Special

The McKinney Cotton Mill is a historic industrial venue with a personality all its own. Spaces such as the Grand Hall, Dye Room and Event Hall give couples different ways to shape the flow of a wedding, while the building's authentic mill character provides an eye-catching setting.

The combination of history, generous event spaces and creative flexibility makes this venue especially interesting for celebrations with a distinctive visual style. A traditional enclosed photo booth would echo the nostalgic setting and give guests a fun printed memento of the occasion.
