What Makes This Venue Special

Event1013 was a distinctive gathering space in Historic Downtown Plano, recognized for its original brick walls, hardwood floors and tall ceilings. Its Main Gallery and Raw Garden gave weddings, parties and corporate gatherings an unusual blend of Texas history and modern event technology.

The venue's historic storefront character was especially well suited to a nostalgic enclosed photo booth and traditional printed photo strips. Event1013 has since announced its closure; this page is retained as a look back at a notable Plano event space rather than a suggestion that it is currently available for new events.
