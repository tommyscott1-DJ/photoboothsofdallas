Planning a Photo Booth for a Dallas Event?

Different events call for different photo booth setups. For a Dallas wedding reception or anniversary party, our Classic Enclosed Photo Booth gives guests a private, nostalgic experience: step behind the curtain, make a few silly faces and leave with printed photo strips. The Open-Air Photo Booth is a strong option for company celebrations, conferences and larger group photos.

What to consider when choosing your booth

  • Guest experience: Choose enclosed for the traditional curtain-style photo booth or open-air when fitting more friends into one picture matters most.
  • Space at your venue: Tell us about the event room and setup area so we can help you select the right option.
  • Personalized keepsakes: Ask about adding your names, event date or company branding to the photo-strip design.
  • Timing: Let us know when guests arrive and when you want photo booth service to begin; setup takes place before the rental period.
